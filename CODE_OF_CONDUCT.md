# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want wadahdb-studio to be a welcoming place for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and boundaries.
- Help others participate, including people new to open source.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For non-sensitive concerns, use GitHub's reporting tools on the relevant content. Do not post private information or allegations publicly. Sensitive concerns should be reported privately using GitHub's abuse-reporting process: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines#reporting-abuse

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate content, lock conversations, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should consider severity, context, and repeated behavior, and respect the privacy of affected people. A participant asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.
